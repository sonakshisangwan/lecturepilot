# LecturePilot Brand Guide

## Vision

LecturePilot helps students learn faster with AI while making studying feel less stressful and more enjoyable.

---

## Brand Personality

- Calm
- Elegant
- Smart
- Friendly
- Organized
- Premium

---

## Design Philosophy

Not flashy.

Not cyberpunk.

Not "generic AI".

Feels like:
- Apple
- Notion
- Pinterest
- Linear

---
